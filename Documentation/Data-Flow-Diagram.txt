TRUST BOUNDARIES
================

TB-1:
Browser -> Juice Shop

TB-2:
Juice Shop -> Local Database

TB-3:
Local Lab -> Internet/Other Systems

No testing traffic may cross TB-3.
No production, third-party, or other computer
may be accessed or tested.


TB-3 MUST NOT be crossed during testing.
